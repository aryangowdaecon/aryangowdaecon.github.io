About

I am a student at Erasmus University Rotterdam completing a double BSc in Econometrics and Operations Research and in Economics and Business Economics, and a BA in Philosophy. I graduate in July 2027. I am currently on exchange at Sungkyunkwan University in Seoul.

My interests are applied microeconometrics and economic policy, especially labour and public economics, and how markets and organisations shape incentives and behaviour. I am also interested in econometric methods for panel data and in the role of machine learning in econometrics. I plan to do full-time empirical research before a PhD in economics.
